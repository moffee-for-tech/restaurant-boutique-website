Do guests have access to the wellness area?

Yes — wellness and spa access are included for all hotel guests. Treatments and massages can be booked at the reception.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Breakfast is included with most room types. For selected offers, it can be added when booking or upon arrival.

Is parking available at the hotel?

Yes, we offer limited on-site parking. Please reserve your space in advance as availability is limited.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in starts from 3:00 PM, and check-out is until 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available upon request.
